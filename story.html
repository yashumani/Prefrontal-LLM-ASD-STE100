WHY PREFRONTAL? · SCIENCE → DESIGN

A brain-inspired name. A governed design.

The prefrontal cortex sits at the front of the brain. It works with other regions to support executive functions: processes that guide behavior toward goals. We use selected functions as design analogies for governed context.

NIMH · The Teen Brain: 7 Things to Know · Cole and Schneider (2007) · The cognitive control network: Integrated cortical regions with dissociable functions

SOFTWARE ANALOGY · These connections explain our proposed design. They do not reproduce brain biology or prove product performance. Brain regions have overlapping functions. The locations below are approximate. This writing is STE-inspired; full compliance has not been checked.

01 · BRAIN FACT

Dorsolateral prefrontal cortex

Upper outer prefrontal surface

Working memory holds information for a current task. Human imaging studies link the dlPFC to maintaining and reorganizing that information. It operates within a wider control network.

D'Esposito et al. (1999) · Maintenance versus manipulation of information held in working memory · Cole and Schneider (2007) · The cognitive control network: Integrated cortical regions with dissociable functions

→

SOFTWARE ANALOGY · PROPOSED

Hold the right task context

Keep the current goal, permitted evidence and task state available. The proposed orchestration code limits the context and work budget.

Limit: A context window is a software resource. It does not reproduce biological working memory or store the whole knowledge base.

02 · BRAIN FACT

Right inferior frontal cortex and connected regions

Lower outer right frontal surface

Inhibition means stopping a response. Human imaging studies identify a network that includes right inferior frontal cortex, a medial frontal area and a deeper brain region. This network helps slow or stop motor responses.

Aron et al. (2007) · Triangulating a cognitive control network using diffusion-weighted MRI and functional MRI

→

SOFTWARE ANALOGY · PROPOSED

Stop at the permission gate

Check permission before retrieval. Stop the request when its scope fails policy. Recheck current records before delivery. Code enforces these gates.

Limit: Stopping a movement and denying data access are different mechanisms. The analogy explains restraint; it does not establish security.

03 · BRAIN FACT

Ventromedial prefrontal cortex

Lower inner prefrontal surface

Valuation means comparing what options are worth. Human lesion studies link vmPFC to consistent preferences. Other studies connect damage here with emotional changes and difficulties in everyday social behavior.

Fellows and Farah (2007) · The role of ventromedial prefrontal cortex in decision making · Anderson et al. (2006) · Impairments of emotion and real-world complex behavior following ventromedial prefrontal damage

→

SOFTWARE ANALOGY · PROPOSED

Keep people in charge of priorities

Owners define priorities and acceptable trade-offs. A human reviews the evidence and approves an exact version. Model scores cannot replace that decision authority.

Limit: Owner-defined rules are explicit software inputs. The product does not gain empathy, emotions or moral judgment from this analogy.

See this decision in our flow ↗

04 · BRAIN FACT

Orbitofrontal cortex

Frontal underside, above the eye sockets

Human lesion research links the OFC to flexible learning from outcomes. It helps interpret new feedback in the context of earlier outcomes when conditions change.

Tsuchida, Doll and Fellows (2010) · Beyond reversal: Flexible learning from probabilistic feedback

→

SOFTWARE ANALOGY · PROPOSED

Review what changes

Record outcomes and source changes. Invalidate withdrawn or expired views. Return changed meaning to human review before reuse.

Limit: A feedback log is not biological learning. Changed meaning still needs approval; a successful output does not make it true.

05 · BRAIN FACT

Frontopolar cortex

Front tip of the prefrontal cortex

A human imaging study linked frontopolar activity to evidence favoring an alternative course of action. The signal tracked the possible benefit of switching from the current choice.

Boorman et al. (2009) · Frontopolar cortex and evidence for alternative courses of action

→

SOFTWARE ANALOGY · PROPOSED

Choose an allowed path

Choose an allowed retrieval path: exact ID, hybrid search or approved relationships. Request clarification when evidence is insufficient. Keep every route within the permitted scope.

Limit: The router follows engineered rules and evaluated signals. This mapping does not claim human planning or an optimal choice.

ONE IMPORTANT CORRECTION

Development is a process.
Not a birthday deadline.

Brain development has no single finish date. NIMH describes broad maturation into the mid-to-late 20s. A 2023 study found adult-level performance on its executive-function tasks around 18–20. Task performance and brain structure measure different things. Neither result sets a universal age of maturity. Social context also affects risk-taking.

NIMH · The Teen Brain: 7 Things to Know · Tervo-Clemmens et al. (2023) · A canonical trajectory of executive function maturation from adolescence to adulthood

The neuroscience explains the naming idea. It does not establish product accuracy, security or cost savings. The pilot must test those outcomes. Working memory means holding information for a current task. Our context vault stores durable records; it is a separate software component.
