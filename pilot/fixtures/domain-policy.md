# Synthetic sales domain policy

This is invented pilot data, not an enterprise source or business approval.
Net revenue is gross sales less discounts and returns for completed orders.
One row represents one order. Cancelled orders are excluded.
Amounts are integer USD cents; display values use USD. Period: September 2026.
The alias "net sales" refers to the canonical net-revenue metric in this domain.
Net revenue alone does not establish profitability or explain a change over time.
